DEAR BOOK FINDER,

If you reached this repository by tracing a paid book back through its public structure, this message is for you.

You looked past the surface. You followed the links, the infrastructure, and the paths most readers will never think to inspect. That instinct — noticing what is exposed, what is hidden, what is assumed, and where the structure can be followed further — is exactly the kind of mind this book was written for.

The fact that a book about manipulation can itself lead a determined reader to examine the system around its sale is not beside the point.

If you found your way here before buying it, then yes: the book is for people like you.

Finding the path is one thing. Understanding what to do with what you find is another.

Use it wisely.

— J. Nasr
