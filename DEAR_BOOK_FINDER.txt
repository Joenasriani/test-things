DEAR FRIEND,

If you have reached this place by tracing the book beyond its storefront, then you have my acknowledgment.

You looked beyond what was presented. You followed the structure, the links, and the paths most readers would never think to inspect. That instinct — to look past the surface, question the obvious, and examine what sits behind the visible layer — is precisely the kind of mind this book was written for.

There is a certain fittingness in discovering a book about manipulation by examining the machinery around how it is presented, sold, and found.

So if you arrived here before buying it, consider this a quiet recognition:

You are already thinking in the direction of the book.

Finding the path is one thing.
Understanding what it reveals is another.

Use it wisely.

With respect,
J. Nasr
