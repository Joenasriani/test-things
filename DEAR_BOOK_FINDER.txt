DEAR FRIEND,

If you have reached this place, however you arrived, you have my acknowledgment.

Perhaps you followed the book beyond its storefront. Perhaps curiosity brought you here. Perhaps you simply noticed a path others might ignore. The route matters less than the instinct behind it.

To look beyond what is presented, to question the obvious, and to examine what sits behind the visible layer is exactly the kind of attention this book was written to reward.

There is a certain fittingness in encountering a book about manipulation from somewhere other than the path laid out for you.

So if finding this place was deliberate, accidental, or something in between, you may already recognize the territory the book explores.

Finding the path is one thing.
Understanding what it reveals is another.

Use it wisely.

With respect,
J. Nasr
