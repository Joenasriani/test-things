# MASTER BOOK → SALE SYSTEM

Use this process for every book. Follow the order exactly.

**FINISH → CHECK → FIX → PRESENT → CONFIRM → LOCK → CONTINUE**

Do not work on later phases before the current phase is finished and confirmed.

## 0. CONTROL
Keep one source of truth for the current book. Track what is confirmed, what is open, what files belong to the book, and what belongs to the bookstore. Do not silently change confirmed work.

## 1. TARGET
Define the strongest buyer: what they care about, what they want to understand or solve, what they search for, what makes them trust or reject the product, and why the book would be worth paying for. The target guides positioning; it must not distort research.

**Stop for confirmation.**

## 2. RESEARCH → ULTIMATE TAXONOMY
Treat the user's input as the starting subject, not the final definition.

Use, where applicable:
- academic research
- scientific research
- documented real-world cases
- historical evidence
- professional evidence
- credible lived/lifestyle experience, clearly separated from proof
- competing theories
- opposing interpretations
- failed explanations
- limitations and unknowns

Build the subject before building chapters:

**INPUT → domains → categories → subcategories → smallest useful units → relationships → conditions → exceptions → boundaries → contradictions → competing explanations → unknowns → applications**

Separate ideas when a change in truthfulness, identity, source independence, visibility, coercion, vulnerability, consent, reversibility or system target changes what the thing is or how it should be classified.

For each important claim classify the evidence clearly. Do not flatten facts, supported interpretations, disputed claims, deductions, hypotheses and anecdotes into one level of certainty.

Actively search for evidence that could weaken the book. Research wins over the planned narrative.

The research phase ends with a stable taxonomy, evidence map, source map, boundaries, exclusions, contradictions, competing explanations, unknowns, relationship map, case candidates and application candidates.

**Stop for confirmation.**

## 3. CONVERT RESEARCH INTO A CASE-STUDY BOOK
Do not turn the taxonomy into a dry database. Build a reader journey from real situations, documented cases, contrasts, failures, exceptions, counterexamples and combined mechanisms.

Create chapters only after the taxonomy is stable:

**TAXONOMY → RELATIONSHIPS → READER JOURNEY → CHAPTERS**

Cases should show:
- what happened
- what is directly observable
- the possible mechanism
- evidence for that interpretation
- alternative explanations
- what the case does not prove
- conditions that matter
- what can reasonably be learned
- related mechanisms

Use cases to test the taxonomy. If real cases expose a missing category, false split, weak label or failed assumption, revise the taxonomy.

The book should train the reader to identify mechanisms, compare frames, challenge assumptions, separate evidence from inference, trace relationships, combine ideas, produce new questions and create new directions.

Keep AI usefulness implicit in the architecture. Do not make the book publicly depend on the label “AI book.” Preserve AI material when it is part of the book; structure the knowledge so humans and AI systems can reason with it effectively.

Where useful include definitions, evidence states, comparisons, boundaries, counterexamples, source links, tables, maps, indexes, cross-references, structured entries and machine-readable data.

**Do not remove existing book content merely to simplify the sale. Add, reconcile or reclassify when research requires it.**

**Stop for confirmation.**

## 4. DESIGN THE BOOK
Design only after content is confirmed.

The design must make the book easier to understand, navigate, reference, compare and reuse. The subject determines typography, mood, density, image treatment, diagrams, cover and page rhythm.

Every graphic must explain something. No generic AI imagery, generic business-book layout, filler graphics or copied visible template.

Make evidence state, taxonomy, cases, hypotheses, relationships and cross-links visually easy to scan. Hypotheses must never look like established findings.

Where applicable prepare PDF, EPUB, cover, sample, workbook, source register, structured data, machine-readable data, publishing information and disclosure.

Audit the final book academically, scientifically, logically, editorially, visually, pedagogically, ethically and for source integrity.

**Stop for confirmation, then lock the book.**

## 5. SELLING REFRAME
Only after the book is locked, find the strongest truthful reason the confirmed target should want it.

Do not sell page count or chapter count as the main value. Sell the intellectual advantage created by the actual book: seeing what others overlook, comparing explanations, finding distinctions, testing assumptions, reframing problems, generating better questions or creating new ideas.

Choose one main selling idea. Everything else supports it.

For **Manipulation — The Fool and the Wise** the locked direction is:
- Emotional hook: **Watch what life repeats and men neglect.**
- Main selling idea: **See the mechanism behind the message.**
- Keep the dark psychology / darker human behavior attraction.
- Keep AI content in the product; do not lead by calling it an AI book.
- Counts are proof of depth, not the promise.

**Stop for confirmation.**

## 6. ONE-PAGE LANDING PAGE
Each book gets one dedicated selling page. The book determines the visible design.

A visitor must quickly understand:
- what it is
- why it matters
- what makes it different
- what they receive
- why it is credible
- the price
- how to buy

Default selling flow:

**BOOK → MAIN REASON TO WANT IT → DEMONSTRATION → PROOF OF DEPTH → WHAT THE READER CAN DO WITH IT → SAMPLE → TRUST → PRICE → BUY**

Keep the page simple, fast, mobile-friendly and visually specific to the book. Avoid generic ecommerce cards, SaaS styling, AI gradients, popups, hidden price, excessive scrolling, repeated CTAs and decorative interactions that delay understanding.

## 7. GOOGLE / SEARCH GATE
Creative design must never damage search visibility.

Check:
- public crawlability
- no accidental `noindex`
- correct `robots.txt`
- one canonical URL
- no duplicate/staging URL competition
- correct redirects
- unique title and description
- real crawlable text
- correct heading structure
- crawlable internal links
- current sitemap
- accurate Book/Product/Offer structured data
- visible price matches structured price
- useful image filenames and alt text
- good performance
- no hidden SEO text, keyword stuffing, doorway pages, fake reviews or mass-generated duplicates

## 8. FINAL SELLING TEST
Test as the ideal buyer, skeptical buyer, first-time visitor, Google visitor, mobile visitor and someone deciding whether the price is justified.

Remove anything that causes confusion, weakens trust, hides value, delays purchase or sounds generic.

## 9. +BOOKSTORE
The bookstore is the discovery layer, not another product page.

For each book show only:

**Large cover → title → very short selling line → compressed utility → visible price/direct purchase**

Cover/title open the dedicated landing page. Price goes directly to payment. Do not insert another generic product-detail page.

The shared bookstore stays bright and neutral. Each book keeps its own authored visual world on its dedicated page.

Adding or updating a book must also update its canonical record, internal link, sitemap/SEO data, structured data, price and availability.

## 10. PUBLISH / TEST
Test the actual production path:

**GOOGLE / +BOOKSTORE → BOOK LANDING PAGE → SAMPLE → BUY → PAYMENT → DELIVERY**

Check real domain, URLs, files, cover, sample, price, payment path, delivery, mobile, desktop, browser compatibility, metadata, sitemap, search visibility, performance and error states.

Do not mark the release complete until the real purchase and delivery path is verified.

## PERMANENT PRODUCT RULE
Every book follows:

**USER INPUT → TARGET → ACADEMIC/SCIENTIFIC/REAL-WORLD RESEARCH → ULTIMATE TAXONOMY → CASE-STUDY BOOK → REASONING-READY DESIGN → SELLING REFRAME → ONE-PAGE SALE → SEARCH-SAFE → +BOOKSTORE → PUBLISH**

The book determines the product. The target determines how it is sold. The landing page expresses both. The bookstore organizes the finished products.
