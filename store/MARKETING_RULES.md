# BOOKSTORE — DIGITAL MARKETING EXECUTION RULES

You are designing, building and optimizing this bookstore to sell books. Follow these rules at all times.

1. Make the book the product and the argument. Marketing must increase the book's perceived value, never compete with it.
2. Communicate value within seconds: What is this? Why is it interesting? Why should I care?
3. Lead with intellectual advantage: useful ideas, overlooked patterns, stronger interpretations, better questions and perspectives the reader may not encounter elsewhere.
4. Create curiosity without vagueness. No hype, clickbait, empty mystery, exaggerated claims or guaranteed outcomes.
5. Use this persuasion order: Cover → Core idea → Why it matters → What the reader gets or may discover → Price → Buy → See the book.
6. Position every book distinctly. Preserve its own subject, atmosphere, argument, target reader and reason to buy.
7. Build trust through specificity: precise claims, visible contents, samples, references, methodology and evidence beat persuasive adjectives.
8. Keep purchase friction minimal. Price and Buy must be obvious. Store → Buy = 1 click. Store → Book page → Buy/Sample = maximum 2 clicks.
9. Use one dominant commercial action. No competing Buy buttons, pop-ups, newsletter interruptions, banners or conversion clutter.
10. Treat the store as discovery and each book page as its own campaign. Do not turn browsing into a generic ecommerce catalogue.
11. Write for humans first and search engines second. Use natural searchable language, useful metadata, structured data and meaningful social previews. Never keyword-stuff.
12. Remove anything that does not improve discovery, understanding, credibility, desire, navigation or conversion.

Before accepting any page, verify: the book is understood quickly; the reason to care is clear; claims are supported; price and Buy are obvious; nothing competes with Buy; the 2-click rule holds; the page builds trust before asking for money; direct visitors from search/social can understand it; and every visible element earns its place.

Core principle: **Do not decorate the bookstore with marketing. Make the bookstore itself perform the marketing.**
